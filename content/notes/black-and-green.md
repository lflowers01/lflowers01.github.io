I started this design with forest green in mind, then tried OLED black. Black gives the ASCII and dither more room, so that's the default now. Forest green is still there in the theme switch.

The palette stays close to green, warm white, and brown. The notes bring in a little paper texture and color without making the whole page look like a desktop window.

The flower is from my old site's favicon. Hover it and it grows from the ground. It felt right to bring that one thing along.
