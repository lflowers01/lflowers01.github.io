Report template / sample structure

Project title

One paragraph explaining what you built, who it is for, and what it does. This is a report template, not a published project.

Date
Month / year
Status
In progress / complete
Role
Your contribution

The problem

Explain the situation, constraints, and what you wanted to improve. Include enough context for someone seeing the work for the first time.

How it works

Describe the main mechanism or system. Use diagrams, code excerpts, or photographs when they explain more than prose.

The process

Show the important decisions, prototypes, and changes. Explain a tradeoff and what you learned from testing.

Results and next steps

Report what actually works, evidence from testing, remaining limits, and what you would change next.

Source and related work

Add real source, demo, references, and download links when the project is published.
