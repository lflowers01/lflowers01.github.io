Draft 4

Website design plan

A green-and-brown personal site built around images, writing, and exploration: a layered composition, a responsive ASCII scene, and direct access to your current work at Purdue.

This revision develops a more spatial composition, names the images and artwork to create, and explains the SoundCloud options. Fonts and the green-primary palette remain available below.

Design direction: green remains primary, with brown surroundings and trim. Use an asymmetric collage of photos, notes, artwork, and content entrances. Keep the language direct and the content college-focused. Remove the global motion/audio strip.

01 / DirectionGreen and brown, with a layered composition.

A sage-green surface, deep green navigation and accents, brown surrounding texture, pale paper reading surfaces, and low-color imagery. Photos, a folder, a notebook, a music sleeve, and unframed ASCII give different parts of the page different forms.

Visitors can explore the composition, bring an item forward, and open a clear reading surface. Ordinary text navigation remains available. Movement supports these interactions rather than becoming a settings feature.

Continuity: retain the earthy palette and window vocabulary from your previous site, while changing the content focus to your current life.

05 / Mobile compositionKeep the imagery; simplify exploration.

Reflow the collage rather than shrinking an entire desktop. Portrait and name appear together, then ASCII, Projects, Notes, music, and the footer. Retain distinct paper/photo shapes and some overlap; keep reading surfaces aligned.

Interaction changes

  • Tapping selects or opens an object; a normal text route is also available.
  • Photo captions and project names remain visible.
  • Long notes use normal reading pages instead of tiny floating windows.
  • ASCII responds to taps without blocking scrolling.
  • If chosen, SoundCloud opens in a full-width player area.
  • Reduced motion is automatic; music starts only when requested.

The collage in this plan already reflows on narrow screens. It is a composition study, not the finished mobile site.

07 / BehaviorWhat the elements do, and how they look in each state.

Dithered images

Appearance: green/cream two- or four-color portrait and small previews; solid 1px frame. Behavior: a labeled Original image link can open the clear source image. Detailed schematics stay undithered.

Use one palette and crop convention across assets. The checker patterns in this plan demonstrate texture only, not a finished portrait.

Interactive ASCII

Appearance: a larger character field integrated into the background. It can depict an ecosystem, abstract flow, or mechanical arrangement. The subject remains open.

Behavior: pointer movement or a tap changes the scene, with a keyboard equivalent. Reduced motion starts still automatically. Ongoing animation gets a local pause action without a global toolbar. The plan shows a static character study only.

Visit counter

Appearance: a small odometer-style number near the lower edge, labeled Visits. Use a text label and number that remain readable without decorative styling.

Behavior: a server stores accepted homepage-load increments and returns the total. It is not labeled unique people. Offline displays Unavailable or a clearly marked last-known value, never a fabricated zero. Preview/local sessions do not write to the production counter.

SoundCloud playlist

Entrance: the record-sleeve image opens a playlist surface. Use SoundCloud's official playlist embed in a green/brown frame and retain an Open on SoundCloud link. A playlist URL is still needed before connecting a real player.

Ads: SoundCloud says embedded tracks can include audio ads when their owner has enabled monetization. Its documented widget parameters do not offer an ad-disable switch. Hiding comments or recommendations does not remove audio ads, so this site cannot promise ad-free SoundCloud playback for every visitor.

Two useful choices

SoundCloud playlist: use the official embed and accept platform/track-controlled ad behavior, or keep a plain outbound playlist link and no embedded player on the homepage.

Ad-free on-site audio: use owned or permitted files in a native player or Webamp, with a separate SoundCloud link. A different skin alone does not make SoundCloud playback ad-free.

Playback controls stay in the actual player, with no global settings bar. No autoplay. The exact player surface follows the choice of music source.

Checked October 18, 2026: SoundCloud's embedded-ad explanation, Widget API and parameters, playlist-player options.

Notes

Appearance: a paper-note entrance on the homepage. The opened notebook has newest-first entries, dates in Departure Mono, body in Verdana, and a chronological archive.

Behavior: one Markdown entry generates its permanent page, archive entry, homepage preview, and feed entry. Editing and publishing remain separate actions. Nothing in the layout depends on having many posts.

Easter egg

Appearance: one secondary graphic or link detail with an unobtrusive clue. Proposed result: a short alternate ASCII scene or hidden page using the same palette.

Behavior: optional, reversible, and available by keyboard/touch. Keep the exact reveal for a later design pass. No forced sound, page takeover, or requirement to solve it to access the site.

Webring and links

Appearance: a quiet full-width footer, with Previous / Purdue Hackers webring / Next as visible text links. A separate row holds selected 88×31 buttons with attribution.

Behavior: normal anchors that work without the interactive layer. Test recognition and follow enrollment instructions only after the eventual site is live.

08 / Content directionStart with where you are now.

The new site centers your college life, current interests, new projects, and ongoing writing. We will not populate the project section with high-school work to fill space.

At launch

  • A short current introduction and Purdue context.
  • Resume, GitHub, and email links you want public.
  • A new portrait or another image you choose.
  • A clear Projects empty state unless current work is ready.
  • A few real notes when you have something to post.
  • A music source, selected photos, and links based on your current taste.

As the site grows

  • College projects, independent builds, or current experiments you choose to publish.
  • Dated process notes and photographs.
  • Personal writing beyond engineering.
  • Additional collections only when you want to maintain them.

The older archive remains in the repository and existing site history. It is not part of this new site's proposed navigation or default content.

Needed later: your current bio, selected photos, up-to-date resume, SoundCloud playlist URL or permitted audio selection, and any current project you actually want featured. We can approve the visual plan before all that content is ready.

09 / Implementation sequenceDesign decisions first, then the full site.

  1. Refine this visual plan.

    Settle palette, font pairing, component treatment, layered composition, image selection, and ASCII subject. No full-site implementation until you approve the plan.

  2. Implement and review the homepage composition.

    Use actual spacing, typography, responsive rules, image treatment, and college-era copy. Review the page at desktop and phone widths before building out the remaining destinations.

  3. Build the content templates.

    Projects, project details, notes, note details, About, and the feed. Use Markdown for authored entries. An empty section remains a designed state.

  4. Add interactive elements and services.

    ASCII interaction, content-window behavior, the easter egg, the chosen music source, and a real visit counter. Use a small server endpoint with atomic increments, bounded requests, rate limits, and clear failure states.

  5. Verify and release to the agreed destination.

    Check actual fonts, links, keyboard/touch behavior, reduced motion, media, content updates, counter concurrency, and production asset paths. Settle the hosting URL and test ring controls before joining. The current site stays unchanged until the release is agreed.

Proposed implementation underneath the visuals

Static HTML/CSS/JavaScript with a small Markdown build step. SoundCloud uses its official widget if selected; Webamp remains an option for direct permitted audio files. Notes and projects stay in source files; browser preferences store theme/motion/volume only. A small Worker/D1 service is one option for the counter; server credentials never enter the browser.

Keep the research and plan during implementation. No archive import or full desktop window manager is assumed. Content objects can open a focused reading window with a shareable location and normal back behavior. Long entries also get normal pages. Choose music continuity before finalizing routing.
